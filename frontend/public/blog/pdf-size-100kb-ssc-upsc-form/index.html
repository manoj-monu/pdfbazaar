Sarkari Jobs (SSC, UPSC, Banking) Ke Liye PDF Compress Karein

Dosto, jab hum **SSC CGL**, **CHSL**, ya **UPSC Civil Services** ke online form bharte hain, toh ek common problem aati hai: **"File size must be between 10KB to 100KB"**.

Agar aapne photo ya documents scan kiya hai, toh wo aksar 1MB ya 2MB ka hota hai. Is post mein main aapko batunga ki bina scanner ke, sirf phone se 100KB PDF kaise banayein.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Launch Compress PDF Tool.
  2. Choose your document from mobile gallery.
  3. "Target Size" box mein **100** likhein.
  4. Unit mein **KB** select karein.
  5. "Compress" button dabayein aur result download karein.

PDFbazaar ka compression algorithm text ko dhundla (blurry) nahi karta, jisse aapka form reject nahi hoga. Ye bilkul safe aur fast hai!
