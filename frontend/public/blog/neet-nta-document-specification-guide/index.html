Navigating NTA NEET 2026 Document Requirements

National Testing Agency (NTA) has released strict guidelines for the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) 2026 registration. For medical aspirants, the registration process is the first critical step towards their MBBS or BDS dreams. Any error in document uploading can lead to the rejection of the application form without a refund. In this guide, we provide a 1000-word deep dive into document preparation for NEET.

Document Checklist and Exact Size Limits

NTA has specified different size and format requirements for various documents. Understanding these is crucial before you start filling the form.

  • Class 10th Passing Certificate: 50KB to 300KB (PDF Format)
  • Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS): 50KB to 300KB (PDF Format)
  • PwBD Certificate: 50KB to 300KB (PDF Format)
  • Citizenship Certificate: 50KB to 300KB (PDF Format)
  • Postcard Size Photo: 10KB to 200KB (JPG/JPEG Format)

How to Handle Multi-Page PDFs for NEET?

If your category certificate or marksheet has multiple pages, you must upload them as a single PDF file. NTA does not allow uploading multiple files for a single field. You can use our Merge PDF tool to combine these pages. If the final merged file exceeds 300KB, use our NEET PDF Compressor to bring it down to the required range.

Preparing the Left and Right Hand Fingers and Thumb Impression

A unique requirement for NEET is the upload of finger and thumb impressions. Aspirants often struggle to convert these impressions into a clear, properly sized file. We recommend:

  1. Take a clear impression on white paper using blue or black ink.
  2. Photograph the impression in bright, indirect light.
  3. Use our Image to PDF tool to convert the photo into the required document format.

Aadhaar Card Preparation: Password Removal

Many candidates download their e-Aadhaar from the UIDAI website, which is always password-protected. NTA servers cannot open protected files. If you upload a locked PDF, your application will be rejected. You MUST remove the password using our Aadhaar Password Remover before uploading.

Final Tips for NEET Aspirants

Always keep a digital folder with all your documents properly named and sized before you sit down to fill the NEET form. This reduces stress and the chance of errors.
