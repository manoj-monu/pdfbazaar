PDF Se Image Extract ya Convert Kaise Kare (PDF to JPG Online)?

Aksar humare paas aisi PDF files aati hain jisme bohut badhiya photos ya designs hote hain. Ya fir, kisi ne WhatsApp par apna Aadhaar Card PDF bana kar bhej diya hai aur aapko uska print nikalne ya form me upload karne ke liye wapas 'Image' yani JPG format chahiye.

Screenshots lene se image ki phat jati hai (quality zero ho jati hai). Screenshot crop karne me bhi time waste hota hai.

Do Tarike ke Use Cases - Convert vs Extract:

PDFbazaar ka PDF to JPG Tool aapki dono practical problems ko solve karne ke liye 2 options deta hai:

  • 1. Page to JPG (Convert Everything): Agar PDF me 10 pages ki book hai, toh tool un sabhi 10 panno ka screen-capture lega ultra-high HD resolution mein, aur aapko 10 alag-alag photos (.JPG) de dega. Good for: Notes or WhatsApp Image sharing.
  • 2. Extract Images (Nikalna): Agar PDF file me text bhi likha hai aur beech me ek choti photo chipki hui hai.. Toh tool sirf us photo ko asli original size aur quality me baahar cheer (extract) nikal dega bina kisi text ke. Good for: Form filling and Designer assets.

Steps to Convert PDF documents to JPG Image Format:

  1. Click karein hamare secure "PDF to JPG" panel tool par.
  2. Upload button daba ke PDF select karein (Phone memory ya computer folder se).
  3. Screen par Right taraf dono options dikhenge - "Page to JPG" aur "Extract Images". Apna required option chunein.
  4. Download Process press kr dijiye.
  5. Aapko ek .ZIP file ya seedhi image mil jaegi jisse aap sidhe Gallery me Access kar sakte hain!
