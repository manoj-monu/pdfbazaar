Why Does SSC Have Strict File Size Limits?

The SSC portal handles millions of applications every year. To manage their database and ensure that the portal remains responsive during peak hours, they set specific size limits for every file. For a standard PDF marksheet, the limit is usually between 100KB to 500KB. For photographs and signatures, the limits are even tighter (20KB to 50KB).

When you scan a document using a high-quality scanner or even your mobile camera, the resulting file is often in the 2MB to 10MB range. Uploading such a large file is impossible. Simply renaming the file or changing the extension won't help. You need a specialized tool that can "down-sample" the image data and strip away metadata without making the text illegible. If you are seeing the 'File Size Exceeded' message, follow these steps to fix it immediately:

  1. Check the Exact Limit: Read the instructions on the SSC portal carefully. Note down whether the limit is 100KB, 200KB, or 500KB.

Technical Tips for High-Quality Document Scans

To ensure your documents are accepted during the Document Verification (DV) stage, follow these scanning tips:

  • Resolution: Scan your documents at 200 DPI (Dots Per Inch). This is the "sweet spot" for SSC portals—it keeps the file size low while ensuring the text is sharp.
  • Color Mode: Unless strictly required, scan your marksheets in 'Grayscale' or 'Black and White'. Color scans are significantly larger in size. If you have a color scan, you can use our PDF to Grayscale tool to fix it.
  • Margin Trimming: Ensure there is no extra white space around your certificates. Use a cropping tool or our PDF Crop tool to remove unnecessary borders.

  1. Merge the files using Merge PDF tool.
